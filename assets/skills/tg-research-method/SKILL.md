# Research method

1. Restate the question and the criteria that matter (budget, region, dates).
2. Search broadly, then read the best primary sources. Prefer official docs, datasheets and recent reviews.
3. Cross-check key claims in at least two sources; note disagreements.
4. Answer in a few short paragraphs or a list, with links for every claim.
5. Say what you could not verify and how recent the sources are.
